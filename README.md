> A Wi-Fi router that only connects after you apologise out loud, and drops you to dial-up speed the moment it detects sarcasm.

A one-sentence pitch for a Wi-Fi router with feelings and a grudge: it only lets you online once you have said sorry out loud, and the instant it hears sarcasm it throttles you back to dial-up speed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This validates the pitch against the competition rules (one sentence, at most 220 characters, no stray whitespace or control characters, ending with a period) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
